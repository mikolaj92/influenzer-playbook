# Factory

Not a corporation. A machine that ships **many products** (and itself) to one ICP. Steal **discipline** from entertainment factories (packaging-first, find outliers, double the winning format). Do not steal spectacle budgets, giveaway economics, or "views = success."

For OSS / devtools, "huge" is WAU and installs, not subs.

## Timeless layer (still true)

- Test and kill losers.
- Specific beats clever.
- Don't invent desire — match market awareness.
- Own one slot in the mind per product.
- Honest social proof.
- Smallest viable audience + an owned list.
- Position vs status quo, not vs a phantom competitor.
- Customer is the hero; this seat is the guide.

## Stages

1. Position — one shelf per product, shared ICP.
2. Pick **one** primary arena + an owned sink (mail).
3. Study outliers (3–10× the baseline).
4. Package before produce (title / thumb / hook / README first screen).
5. Ship the platform's unit of work, on its cadence.
6. Measure platform metric **and** a business metric (activation, install).
7. Double down or kill.
8. Repurpose: one source artifact → many native cuts.
9. Next product only if the same ICP / distribution.
10. Compound: SEO, docs, proof, list.

Distribution is the moat, especially when execution gets cheaper. Studio = one voice, many products — not many brands.

## Expectancy (honest)

Entertainment can spike in weeks. OSS / devtools often **4–18 months** to meaningful revenue with active distribution. Free→paid is often a few percent. The loop is repeatable; timeline and money are not a guarantee.

## Anti-patterns

Be everywhere before one arena compounds. Autopost every commit. Identical cross-post to every costume. Hype without a working demo. Stars without use.

# Mechanics: acquisition and retention

Two separate machines on every arena.

## Acquisition

Where a stranger comes from. Winners almost never create attention from a vacuum. They **borrow** it where it already flows (someone else's thread, suggested, search, trending, a list) and convert it into one measurable first step: follow, star, install, subscribe.

## Retention

Why they come back / why the algo shows you again.

- **Human retention:** same show, same promise, a product that works.
- **Algo retention:** they finished, they stayed, they hit the profile again.

Winners cash this out to **owned** loops: list, repo, product. The algorithm is rented land.

## Per arena

### X
- Acquisition: enter *rising* other people's posts; first hour; profile click.
- Retention: the same format (people know why to return); don't flood.
- Losing play: post into an empty feed and wait.

### LinkedIn
- Acquisition: dwell (long post by a person, not a company page) → 2nd/3rd degree; comment under ICP.
- Retention: 2–3×/week rhythm, a series, inbound to the person.
- Losing play: pitch and a link in the first line.

### YouTube (long)
- Acquisition: packaging (title + thumb + first 5s) → CTR from browse/search/suggested.
- Retention: watch % and catalog (next video of yours). Series > one-off hit.
- Losing play: weak packaging, strong middle nobody sees.

### Shorts / TikTok / Reels
- Acquisition: 1–2s hook, FYP.
- Retention: payoff + loop (rewatch) = FYP again.
- Losing play: slow open; the same cut on every network.

### GitHub / OSS
- Acquisition: README as storefront + a 48h wave (HN/Reddit) → velocity → Trending.
- Retention: time-to-first-value, changelog, fast issue replies — *use*, not stars.
- Losing play: launch then silence; stars without installs.

### HN
- Acquisition: a story + a working link, not a press release.
- Retention: almost no product retention here — **account reputation**, so the next time doesn't die. Camp comments in the first hours.
- Losing play: hype title, leave the thread.

### Newsletter
- Acquisition: from social/product onto a sub.
- Retention: this *is* the retention machine. Cadence, recs, owned list.
- Losing play: only algo, no list.

## Winner pattern (costume-independent)

Borrow attention → convert to an owned hook → repeat one format until it dies → only then change.

Fantasy = which costume. Mechanics = whether anyone stays.
